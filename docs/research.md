# Why this target

Checked 2026-10-21.

- **The incumbent.** Aptean Respond is case and complaints management software for financial services firms. Its product page (https://www.aptean.com/en-AU/solutions/customer-experience/products/aptean-respond) offers a demo and a pricing request; no price is published. Review listings say to contact the vendor and that the price depends on users, modules and deployment.
- **The rule that makes every firm need one.** ASIC RG 271 sets maximum IDR timeframes, the five business day exception, delay notifications and what an IDR response must contain. ASIC IDR data reporting requires every AFS licensee with retail clients and every credit licensee to report complaints every six months.
- **Who buys.** Mortgage and finance brokers, insurance brokers, financial advisers, lenders and credit providers in Australia; financial advice providers and other FSCL members in New Zealand.
- **Rivals seen.** Civica Complaints Management, Sprinklr Complaints Management, GRC platforms such as 6clicks configured as a register. None publishes a price for this module.
- **Import path.** The IDR data file every licensee already prepares for ASIC.
- **Out of scope.** Deciding complaints, sending letters, lodging with ASIC, social listening.
