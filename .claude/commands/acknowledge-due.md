# acknowledge-due

Complaints not yet acknowledged, with the business day they were due.

Read fresh data. Never invent a complaint, a date, a remedy or command output. Complaints hold personal information: keep output inside the business.

```bash
node scripts/complaints.mjs acknowledge-due --json
```

Replace angle-bracket placeholders with what the person told you; leave out optional flags they did not ask for. If a name or reference is ambiguous, list the candidates and ask. RG 271 expects acknowledgement within 24 hours or one business day. Offer to record the acknowledgement once the person confirms it went. Nothing here sends a letter, an email or a report.
